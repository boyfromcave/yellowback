## 1. Finding

Wave 3 showed that the YED claim/cancel mechanics carry the module's fund-loss paths: a path-(b) claim sizes the claimant cap at `pClaim` with no margin, a single attestor-set member (`attestorCancelThreshold` 1) can cancel the claimant intent while the owner's residual intent releases unconditionally (W3-Y-1), with the residual role taken by first owner-hash match the extraction reaches up to 100 % of the collateral (W3 03/NEW-1), the same seat can cancel every claim forever (W3-Y-2), and the cancel loop can exhaust a set's S-3 `rateLimitBps` budget (R-1d). yb-calibration has no model of any of this. Its simulator's vault book (`sim/vaults.py`) is the v3 book — claims only after grace, no `claimDelay`, no claimant/residual intents, no cancel, no CAN-1 refund; the upgrade kernels that do exist (`claim_split`, `claim_accounting("cancel")`, `set_create_adoptable`) are called only by the golden replay, which replays the W3-Y-1 sequence as **valid** and so pins the vulnerable behaviour as correct; `rateLimitBps`/`epochUsed` occur only in the vendored codec; `set_create_adoptable` mirrors the node's adoption check including its R-1d gap (no rate pin).

The constants whose reach those mechanics set were therefore chosen without them. (1) **θ (`claimThresholdBps`) 12,500** came from D-RD-COL-6's v3 holder-claimant model; at the path-(a) boundary a path-(b) claim leaves residual = (1 − 1/θ)·C, so moving θ from 11,000 to 12,500 raised the minimum W3-Y-1 extraction from **9.09 % to 20.00 %** of the collateral (23,810 → 59,524 YEC for D = $100,000 at `pClaim` $0.42) while making path (b) rarer (reachable at a `pClaim/pEmerg` spread above **4.76 % → 19.05 %**). Neither side of that trade was in the evidence. (2) **`emergencyRatioBps` KEEP 10,500** (D-RD-COL-7: "on real crashes it does not matter: RED-4(a) closes them first") holds only without a cancel; with one seat, path (b) is the extraction path. (The tool's G3-DN4 did find that path (b) pays at the higher price and proposed "pay (b) at pEmerg" — W3-Y-5's fix — but framed it as claimant economics, never escalated.) (3) **The 10,000-YEC bond floor's "capture cost"** is priced as taking 7 of 9 seats (139,650 YEC), while the cheapest fund-affecting attack needs **one** member of the 15-member OPEN set: a 10,000-YEC bond (~$4,000 at $0.40), returned 14 days after exit, never slashed for cancelling. (4) `claimDelay` 576, the cancel/unlock thresholds and `rateLimitBps` have no registry entry and no study (W4-K-1). D-MP-1's re-run plan models the cancel limit as "one extra 12 h round per griefed vault" — correct only if the planned cancel limit (CAN-2/C5) lands first, an ordering nothing records. **W4 03/new-1:** the upgrade plan says "`ybcal` derives them in P1/P4" for `delay`, `rateWindow`, `rateLimit`, `livenessWindow`, `BRIDGE_MAX_AGE` and `CLAIM_DELAY` (O-13) and "`yb-calibration` sizes `rateLimitBps` with cancelled claims counted" (S-3); none of these has a registry entry or a study, so a P8 reviewer relying on the plan would believe they are calibrated.
